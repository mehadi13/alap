\# ALAP — Launch Roadmap



\## Business Positioning



\### Brand



\*\*আলাপ (ALAP)\*\*



\### Positioning



> \*\*Business Automation \& Digital Solutions\*\*



ALAP helps businesses identify repetitive/manual processes, design better digital workflows, and implement automation, AI, software, integrations, and digital tools.



\### Core Promise



> \*\*Tell us what takes too much time. We'll turn it into a digital solution.\*\*



Bangla:



> \*\*আলাপ করুন। সমস্যাটা বলুন। আমরা সমাধান তৈরি করি।\*\*



\---



\# 1. Launch Strategy



The biggest mistake would be:



> Build a large platform → launch → wait for customers.



Instead:



> \*\*Validate → Sell → Deliver manually → Learn → Productize → Automate → Scale\*\*



The first version of ALAP should therefore have \*\*two parallel tracks\*\*:



\### Track A — Business



Get real customers and deliver real automation projects.



\### Track B — Platform



Build the ALAP platform that gradually replaces the manual business processes.



This means ALAP itself becomes the first customer of its own platform.

\# 8. Phase 3 — Customer Discovery



\### Duration



\*\*2–4 weeks\*\*



This phase happens while the Concierge MVP is running.



Talk to real businesses.



Target:



\*\*30–50 conversations\*\*



Ask:



1\. What tasks take too much time?

2\. What do employees repeatedly do?

3\. What information is copied manually?

4\. What customer questions are repeated?

5\. What reports are prepared manually?

6\. What happens through Facebook/WhatsApp?

7\. What processes depend on Excel?

8\. Where do mistakes frequently occur?

9\. What software do they currently use?

10\. What would they pay to eliminate the problem?



\### Important Rule



Do not ask:



> "Do you want AI?"

> "What takes too much time?"

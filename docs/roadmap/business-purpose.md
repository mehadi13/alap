## 💡 Launch & Growth Strategy

The key principle of ALAP's operational strategy:

> **Validate → Sell → Deliver manually → Learn → Productize → Automate → Scale**

To execute this strategy, ALAP operates with **two parallel tracks**:

### Track A — Business Delivery
Engage real client businesses, audit their operations, and deliver custom business automation projects (CRM setups, API integrations, data sync pipelines, automated reporting).

### Track B — Platform Development
Build the ALAP software platform (`frontend`, `client-app`, `backend`) that standardizes and automates ALAP's own lead intake, proposal generation, client management, and project operations.

> **ALAP itself is the first customer of its own platform.**

## 🎯 Target Audience & Industry Segments

1. **E-Commerce & Retail Merchants**: Automating inventory syncing, order confirmation webhooks, customer notification dispatch via SMS/WhatsApp/Discord.
2. **Service Agencies & Consultancies**: Client onboarding workflows, lead capture, automated meeting scheduling, project tracking.
3. **SMEs & Growing Enterprises**: Data migration between legacy spreadsheets and modern cloud databases, automated invoice processing, custom internal administrative portals.

# Client & User Manual — ALAP (আলাপ)

This guide explains how prospective clients and business owners interact with ALAP through the public marketing website (`http://localhost:3000`).

---

## 🌐 1. Public Site Navigation & Language Selection

### Language Switcher (EN / BN)
- Click the language toggle button (**English** / **বাংলা**) in the top navigation bar at any time.
- All core sections — including hero headings, service cards, detailed solution pages, process steps, and call-to-action forms — instantly render in your preferred language with natural typography (`Tiro Bangla`).

## 📞 3. Requesting a Consultation or Callback

### Option A: Standard Consultation Form
1. Click **Talk to ALAP** or navigate to `/contact`.
2. Fill in your Name, Email, Phone Number, and Company Name.
3. Select your preferred **Communication Channel**:
   - **Message**: Leave a detailed text description of your process/problem.
   - **Meeting**: Request a video consultation.
   - **Call**: Request a phone call.
   - **Voice Note**: Record or upload a voice message explaining your requirement in detail.
4. Click **Submit Consultation Request**.

### Option B: Quick "Request a Call Back"
1. Click the **Request a Call Back** button on the homepage or header.
2. Enter your Name, Phone Number, and a short message.
3. Click **Call Me Back**. An instant alert will be dispatched to the ALAP team via Discord and logged in the admin queue.

---

## 🎙️ 4. Recording & Submitting Voice Notes

1. On the consultation form, select **Voice Note** as your channel.
2. Click **Start Recording** to record your voice note directly using your microphone, or choose **Upload Audio File** (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`).
3. Listen back to your recorded message using the preview audio player before sending.
4. Submit the form. Your voice note will be stored securely and reviewed by the ALAP technical team.
